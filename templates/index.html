🎯 What it does

Guides you through 30 questions across the six NIST CSF 2.0 functions and produces a prioritised risk register with maturity ratings and recommendations.

👤 Who it's for

SME owners, managers, and IT leads who need to understand their cybersecurity risk posture without a dedicated security team or expensive consultancy.

⏱️ How long it takes

Approximately 10–15 minutes. No prior cybersecurity knowledge is needed — every question is written in plain English.

📊 What you get

A risk score and maturity level for each NIST CSF function, an overall risk rating, prioritised recommendations, and an Excel export compatible with Power BI.

How it works

Your Answers → NIST Control Mapping → Risk Formula (L × I) → Apply Weights → Risk Score + Maturity → Recommendations → Export to Power BI
